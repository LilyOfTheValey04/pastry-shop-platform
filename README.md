# Pastry Shop Platform

An integrated digital platform designed to connect independent cafés, bakeries, and pastry shops with their customers through a modern, fast, and user-friendly web application.

## Overview

The platform aims to support small local businesses by improving their online visibility and providing accessible digital tools for managing their daily operations.

At the same time, it offers customers a convenient way to discover local places, explore menus, place orders for pickup, and make table reservations — all in one place.

## The Problem

Small independent businesses often struggle with limited online visibility, expensive marketing channels, and a lack of affordable digital tools.

Customers, on the other hand, frequently rely on scattered information across social media platforms, phone calls, and separate websites to discover local places, check menus, or place orders.

**Pastry Shop Platform aims to bridge this gap** by providing a centralized solution that benefits both businesses and their customers.

## Target Users

The platform serves two main user groups:

### Business Users
- Owners and managers of independent cafés, bakeries, pastry shops, and tea houses.
- Restaurant staff responsible for processing customer orders and reservations.

### Customers
- People looking for convenient ways to order coffee, pastries, and other products for pickup.
- Customers interested in discovering independent local businesses.
- People looking for places to meet, work, or socialize.

## Core Features (MVP)

### Customer Features
- **User Authentication** – Registration and login using email and password.
- **Business Discovery** – Browse available cafés, bakeries, and pastry shops.
- **Business Profiles** – View business information, opening hours, photos, and digital menus.
- **Product Browsing** – Explore products, descriptions, and prices.
- **Pickup Orders** – Add products to a shopping cart and place orders for pickup at a selected time.
- **Table Reservations** – Submit reservation requests by selecting a date, time, and party size.

### Business Management Features
- **Business Dashboard** – Manage business information, opening hours, and profile details.
- **Menu Management** – Add, edit, and manage products and prices.
- **Order Management** – View incoming pickup orders and update their statuses.
- **Reservation Management** – Review incoming reservation requests and confirm or reject them.

## Planned Enhancements

Future development may include:

- Personalized recommendations based on customer preferences.
- Loyalty programs and reward points.
- Favourite businesses and saved places.
- Group ordering with individual payments.
- Special offers and location-based promotions.
- Business analytics and sales reports.
- Online payments and integration with existing point-of-sale systems.
- Real-time notifications about orders and reservations.
